# Revora

**Revora** is a community-driven platform where developers and creators can showcase projects, receive structured peer reviews, and improve through actionable feedback

## Features

* 📂 **Project Showcase** — Share your projects with the community and highlight your work.
* ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews** — Receive detailed, structured feedback from fellow builders.
* 💬 **Discussions & Comments** — Engage in meaningful conversations around projects and ideas.
* 👍 **Recommendations** — Discover high-quality projects curated by the community.
* 👥 **User Profiles** — Build your developer identity and showcase your contributions.
* 🔍 **Discover New Projects** — Explore innovative creations across different domains.
* 🏆 **Community Reputation System** — Earn credibility through valuable reviews and contributions.
* 📊 **Project Analytics** — Track engagement, ratings, views, and community interactions.
* 🎯 **Personalized Feed** — Get project recommendations tailored to your interests.
* 🤝 **Collaboration Opportunities** — Connect with developers, designers, and creators for future projects.
* 🚀 **Launch & Feedback Hub** — Validate ideas, gather insights, and iterate faster.
* 🛡️ **Moderation & Quality Control** — Maintain a constructive and professional environment.
